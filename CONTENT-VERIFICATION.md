# Content Verification Checklist

Before publishing this website as the official temple website, verify these items with temple management or reliable primary sources:

- [ ] Exact official temple name in Hindi and English
- [ ] Exact postal address
- [ ] Exact Google Maps pin/place
- [ ] Official phone number(s)
- [ ] Official email address, if any
- [ ] Official YouTube channel URL
- [ ] Official Facebook/Instagram URLs, if any
- [ ] Darshan opening/closing timings
- [ ] Puja/abhishek timings and rules
- [ ] Mahashivratri schedule
- [ ] Sawan/Sawan Monday schedule
- [ ] Temple history and establishment date
- [ ] Founder/establishment story
- [ ] Any claims involving local religious/cultural harmony
- [ ] Donation/payment information
- [ ] Any government registration/recognition claims
- [ ] Copyright/permission for all photographs and videos

## Important

The files under `src/assets/images/` are **representative placeholders**, not official temple photographs.

Replace them only with photographs that the temple/rights holder has authorized for website use.
